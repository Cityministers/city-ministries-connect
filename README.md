# City Ministers

We are going to be creating a site called "City Ministers" 
The site will help users post their spiritual gifts and users message the poster.

Our site will start off with 1 splash page and 3 tutorial click through pages with instructions on how it works. Our splash page will have the following
1. Banner with Portland, OR 97006 (magnifying search icon, so users can punch in a new city or zip code).
2.  At least 3  Icons representing types of ministries posted like: Coffee Chat (Cup icon), Ride Share (car icon), and Free Clothes (shirt icon). The icons will be on a map to show their location.
3. At the bottom will be a button "Start Your Ministry" below it in small print, Create Account and. Sign in. 

Create 2 types of color schemes and icons with square containers I can choose from.

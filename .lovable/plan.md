# Remaining work: generate the 20 languages + connect posts and ideas

## Where we are

The groundwork is done: every page's text is wrapped and translation-ready, the language globe sits next to "Start Your Ministry", the logo moved beside the menu, and the post-translation engine (with its memory table) is built. The app is currently back to building cleanly.

## Remaining steps

1. **Generate the 20 language files** — one batch job that translates every app string (~350+) into Spanish, Portuguese, French, Korean, Vietnamese, Russian, Chinese, Arabic, Tagalog, Haitian Creole, Amharic, Somali, Ukrainian, Hindi, Japanese, Swahili, German, Italian, Polish, Farsi. One-time AI usage drawn from the workspace balance, spread across ~70 small calls with automatic retries. After this, switching languages is free — the translations are stored files, not per-visit AI calls.
2. **Connect post auto-translation to the screen** — ministry, need and church post cards show the title/description in the reader's language with a "See original" toggle; each unique post is translated once, then remembered (tiny per-post AI cost, no per-visit cost).
3. **Reader's language for ideas and recommendations** — the ministry-ideas generator already accepts the reader's language; wire it up, and run the "people/posts you should meet" one-line reasons through the same translation memory.
4. **Verify** — switch to Spanish and Arabic in a phone-sized preview, check right-to-left layout, a translated post, and translated ministry ideas; then confirm English is untouched.

## About the credits

- The ~22 credits used so far were build-mode work (rewiring the pages) — none of it was translation.
- Remaining costs: one-time generation of the language files (a few credits at most, drawn from the workspace balance) plus tiny per-post translation charges later as visitors read posts. Viewing the app in another language costs nothing.
- Your workspace balance is ~405 credits, so this is well covered.
